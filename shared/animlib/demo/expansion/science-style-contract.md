# Two original science motion proofs

Authorized design: the expansion design and science-proofs brief define two
separate 5–8 second proofs. No new renderer or scene graph is required.

## Artem Kirsanov — synthetic events to a population state

Reference: QHj9uVmwA_0, event/rate passage around 3:54–4:23. Dark blue field,
five wide rows, salmon/green/purple/yellow/cyan identities. Smooth traces retain
their identity while the construction compresses horizontally to make space for
matching vector entries. Original proof: normalized Gaussian contributions from
explicit synthetic events are exposed before their sum; a shared time cursor
selects the same time on every trace and the five selected rates become a vector.
Persistent objects: events, summed curves, row colors and order, seconds domain.
The rate scale is shared across rows. Event units are seconds; rate units s^-1.
7.5 seconds, final 1.3-second reading hold. Reject row swaps, fake rate data,
disconnected formulas, independent movement, or unreadable tiny vector entries.

## Visually Explained — a weight changes a prediction and its error

Reference: 3bvM3NyMiE0, 3:16–3:58; linked channel only. Charcoal field, yellow
sigmoid, pale blue positive and salmon negative observations, sparse grey axes,
monospaced upright feature/weight labels. Original proof: w rises from .6 to1.4
in p=sigma(wx-2), x=2 stays fixed, p rises, the white dashed vertical residual to
y=1 contracts. Local squared-error annotation names that exact residual, then
shows the final computed value. Samples remain spatially fixed. Dashed connector
and curve use real stroke geometry/reveal. 7 seconds, final1.4-second hold.
Reject a changing formula with no visible input/output link, fake dashed texture,
moving data samples, nonmatching prediction/error values, or serif word labels.
