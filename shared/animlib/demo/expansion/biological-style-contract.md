# Biological mechanism proof contract

Two separate native proofs retain separate purposes and palettes. The transcription
proof uses dark space, cyan/teal DNA rails, four consistently colored shaped base
plates, warm RNA backbone, and a muted translucent purple catalytic channel. A
local template and 5′/3′ label establish direction; incoming nucleotides dock one
at a time. The paired strand opens before growth begins. The camera stays close
and moves only enough to establish depth. The shell is original illustrative
geometry, explicitly captioned; it is not a deposited polymerase reconstruction.

The scientific representation proof uses the deposited 1CRN conformer, actual
branched atomic bonds, CA residue beads, and a same-coordinate Gaussian envelope.
Sequence colors and anchor positions persist across representations. It shows no
invented folding trajectory. The surface is a schematic representation, not a
solvent-excluded surface. Neither proof claims cinematic parity with Nucleus or
atomic chemistry from its base glyphs.

Both proofs must fit 16:9 and 4:3, keep labels local and readable, and hold the
final result. All geometry is native; no reference image is an asset. Inspect
ordered output frames as well as individual full frames. Independent review is
the acceptance gate after implementation evidence, not the author's tests.
