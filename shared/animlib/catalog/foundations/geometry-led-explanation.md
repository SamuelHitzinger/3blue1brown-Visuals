# Geometry-led explanation

Start with the learner's question and one concrete example. Make the relation inspectable before naming the abstraction. Every causal claim needs a visible correspondence, comparison or change in quantities and geometry; a caption alone is not evidence.

Use the established dark field, mathematical notation and restrained stable semantic colors. Preserve requested subject fidelity and explicit user references. Rich molecular geometry may be appropriate; a fixed slide template or maximum surface detail is not the style objective.

Introduce notation near its referent. Keep the same object identifiable as it changes, group parts that move together, then hold the result long enough to inspect. Remove obsolete helpers before the next inference. Delay answers until the approved reveal; do not let labels or coloring expose a later conclusion prematurely.

Use the [reusable explanatory motion guide](../../docs/explanatory-motion.md) for cross-domain methods and the [current scene craft](../../../../backend/prompts/scene-craft.md) for production rules. These guidelines complement the full injected host contract; catalog documents do not override viewing mode, timing or interaction requirements.

Verify the visual argument without reading hidden metadata, consistent color/identity, honest source claims, a clear next inference, and legibility through both the operation and reading hold. Historical proof acceptance only applies to the inspected artifact and scope.
