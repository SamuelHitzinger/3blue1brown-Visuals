# Sampled state, traces and projected trajectories

Inputs: aligned sample times and values, explicit units, missing-data intervals, and a stated 3×N projection when reducing dimension. Establish whether values are measured, calculated or synthetic before interpreting them.

The [sampled-state guide](../../docs/sampled-state.md) documents `s.sampledState`, interpolation, gaps and topology-preserving path prefixes. Bind markers, traces and readouts to the same knots and data. A line connecting across missing samples suggests unsupported continuity; leave gaps explicit.

For a projected neural trajectory, explain the axes/projection and preserve time correspondence with the source traces. Do not imply a recovered manifold, learned embedding or causal dynamics from an authored projection. For a residual, one prediction function must drive the curve, selected predicted point and vertical difference.

Reveal the observation or state before its derived summary. Verify interval boundaries, exact knot values, missing runs, reverse seeks and aligned readouts. Sampled-state helpers do not fit scientific models, extrapolate data or validate the experimental claim.
