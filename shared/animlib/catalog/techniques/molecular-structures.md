# Molecular representation and spatial mechanisms

Choose the representation from the question. Atomic bonding needs explicit atoms/bonds; protein architecture may use a residue backbone; an enclosing surface can show overall shape but cannot establish atomistic contact. Keep schematic anatomy and deposited coordinates distinguishable.

Read relevant resource entries and the returned model publication evidence before making structure-dependent narration claims. For a known crambin representation use an allowlisted fixture. For another deposited molecule research its accession and use the supported PDB publication path. Do not quietly substitute crambin for an unrelated protein.

Preserve common units, origin, chain names, gap records and stable part IDs. Rigid transformations and uniform scale retain deposited geometry. Separate subunits can move independently only when the model exposes those parts. Domain hinges or peptide motion need an explicit authored model; imported static parts do not supply a rig or physical simulation.

The [molecular guide](../../docs/molecules.md), [helper contracts](../../docs/helpers-and-assets.md), and [reference](../../docs/reference.md) define host preparation versus scene APIs. Readiness of a source or package does not imply that it is loaded in this job.

Use neutral context and local selected detail, maintaining atom-element colors where they carry identity. Attach labels to recorded landmarks using the returned frame; do not invent active-site labels from a representative centroid. Use sections, transparent context or companion views only when they expose the relationship honestly.

Verify source selection, recorded gaps, representation claims, bond/part identity and coordinate alignment. Inspect motion interiors and local labels from the actual camera. Schematic docking or folding must remain labeled as authored illustration unless measured/calculated evidence supports a stronger claim.
