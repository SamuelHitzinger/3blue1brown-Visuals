No sampled frame gives evidence of obvious solid penetration, but a full continuous collision audit was not performed.

The repair changes the single wavy ribbon of attempt 0 into repeated surface loops and increases lobe separation. This is a meaningful improvement; describing the result as having no domains or no depth would be inaccurate. However, the backbone remains constrained to `top(r,a)` of the shell with a small offset. It does not form independently visible packed coils/turns that organize the larger body. In native setup/end, the visible ribbon reads chiefly as looping surface decoration. This is why the complex-fold requirement remains weak despite the successful cavity construction. The judgment does not rely on a mandatory atom count, polygon count, photorealistic material or a requirement to reproduce a deposited protein.
