# Independent matrix render review method

Use the same case requirements and the reference rubric for every strategy. The reviewer authored Candidate B's prompt additions but does not author or modify generated scenes; strategy names are visible in paths, so this is not a fully blinded comparison. No preferred strategy or desired score is supplied to the visual assessment.

For each completed or failed run:

1. Record run ID, exact source hash, production status, request, selected lesson/scene view and rationale. Reject seeded or hand-edited output as full-pipeline evidence.
2. Inspect native setup, action-interior, consequence and final-hold frames, plus ordered contact sheets and alternate-aspect evidence. Report exact modality and times. Source can substantiate geometry/timing but cannot establish visual success.
3. Give separate dimensional-choice, reasoning, readability, reference-style and technical-delivery verdicts: `ready`, `revise`, or `unverified`. State evidence limits. A failed gate candidate is not delivered; an early failure with no frames leaves visual judgments unverified. Native-frame success does not establish an encoded movie, continuous playback or acoustic synchronization.
4. Cite exact frame paths and timestamps for each substantive finding. Use source calculations only for concrete claims such as common section equations, persistent chain topology, collision/clearance or actual cavity boundaries.
5. Compare the three strategies within the same case only after individual reviews. Keep request scope, rendered quality and production acceptance separate. Never infer quality from primitive counts, prompt names or authoring cost.

Case-specific checks:

- **Sine:** same graph and landmark identities; horizontal x-to-x/2 mapping; unchanged y/amplitude; period from 2pi to pi visibly measured; stable axes; no unnecessary depth or decorative rotation.
- **Saddle:** one actual z=x²−y² surface; central y=0 and x=0 sections belong to that surface; perpendicular directions remain identifiable; moving point/tangent or equivalent construction makes slope sign visible away from the origin. On negative coordinate sides, distinguish direction of motion from derivative sign. Inspect occlusion carefully.
- **Enzyme:** one recognizable connected multi-domain folded structure; pocket belongs to it; compatible approach/docking and differently shaped rejected approach are geometrically coherent; local fit remains visible within global context. A dark patch on a closed surface, generic lobes without requested folded structure, arbitrary disappearance or solid penetration fails the relevant gate. Preserve the explicit schematic/shape-compatibility limitation; do not invent a measured structure or predictive binding claim.

Compare style against the supplied reference's persistent geometric world, local annotations, semantic colors, real operations and reading holds. The enzyme-pocket case asks more cavity/fit complexity than the reference's multiple-coil peptide chapter, so assess its own request rather than demanding an unseen photorealistic standard.

Additional frames, if needed, must render the untouched generated source. Coordinate the original-source render with the harness or parent; do not repair a scene during review.
