All encoded/playback verdicts remain unverified in this review. Native production approval is not a final exported-movie review, and one automatic pass does not override an independently visible issue.

The control cases show that baseline already routes the planar graph and spatial saddle correctly. Both candidates retain those choices, so these runs demonstrate no dimensional-selection gain over baseline. Saddle results differ in tangent readability and scope, with no general candidate advantage established. Most importantly, neither rendered enzyme establishes the requested packed local structures within a larger folded whole. The stronger authoring additions in Candidate B did not resolve that failure in this trial.

The matrix therefore supplies no overall strong-3D winner to promote on visual evidence. It does identify a concrete unresolved failure mode: shape-fit scenes can satisfy local cavity geometry while replacing the requested larger folded protein with a smooth lobed shell and cosmetic trace. The next validation should test that structural requirement explicitly and preserve genuine full-pipeline provenance; a hand scene or relaxed compiler gate cannot replace these outcomes.

These are one trial per strategy/case, with generation and execution variability. They do not estimate success rates or prove prompt causality. Later phases, held-out cases and a fresh blinded reviewer should be reported as separate cohorts rather than pooled into a retroactive Phase 1 victory.
