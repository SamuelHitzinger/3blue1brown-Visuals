# Halving possibilities

## Beat 1 — A question

Content needed: Eight possibilities become four after one question. Keep the same candidates visible.

Narration:
Imagine guessing a hidden card from eight cards. We ask a yes-or-no question that splits the candidates into equal groups. The answer leaves four possible cards.

Invitation (spoken):
What happens after another question that splits the remaining cards equally? Take a moment to work it out.

Pause: 3s

## Beat 2 — The confirmation

Content needed: Four remaining possibilities become two. Reveal the result when the narration confirms it.

Reveal (spoken):
Two remain. Each answer rules out half, because we split the remaining candidates into two equal groups.
