# Dimensional prompt-chain audit

Read-only audit of the current working tree on `amilibsam`; production prompts were not edited. Findings describe likely pressure points, not experimentally proven model causation. The parent agent owns inspection of the newly supplied showcase video.

## Active chain and concrete gaps

1. **Planning is the first dimensional decision.** `backend/src/storyline-prompt.ts:5` assembles teaching guidance, viewing mode and capabilities; `backend/src/agents/editorial.ts:47` adds the planning envelope and shared quality policy for author/reviewer. `backend/src/agents/planning.ts:44` demonstrates only `view.mode: "2d"`, with the generic rationale “why this representation makes the relationship clear.” The validator accepts any nonempty rationale and even omitted views (`planning.ts:125`). This is a plausible 2D anchor and leaves “easier to read” sufficient without stating which spatial relationship survives flattening.
2. **Current policies already request developed 3D.** `backend/prompts/animation-quality.md:111` asks for genuine depth, silhouette, internal arrangement, contacts and deformation. `scene-craft.md:45` asks for complex forms; `story-review.md:10` and `scene-verify.md:47` check them. Simply adding another “prefer 3D” paragraph duplicates existing intent. The missing stronger artifact is a scene-specific visible spatial obligation carried from plan to render.
3. **The machine contract only observes a camera.** `backend/src/agents/scene-plan.ts:17` explicitly says it does not prove meaningful depth. A nonzero yaw/pitch or perspective satisfies a 3D plan (`:19–27`), even for flat/generic geometry. It does not reject an unnecessary 3D treatment of a planar claim. Keep this as a technical check; do not mistake it for the experiment's acceptance metric.
4. **Spatial design can be diluted at handoff.** Only current/previous/next full plans travel to scene authors; later scenes reduce to titles/purposes (`planning.ts:149–154`). Lasting geometry requirements must therefore live in shared entity meanings and repeat in consuming scenes. Scene input includes title/topic, not the original source-document/image bundle (`generator.ts:148–152`), so source-specific appearance must survive in written plan/specification. An unchanged reference URL is not inspected visual evidence.
5. **A correct view flag can still produce weak volume.** Existing matched-run notes record candidate RNA with real parametric geometry but a broad flat-looking slab (`data/prompt-quality-comparison-20261011/review-notes.md:15`); v2 has asymmetric domains yet yaw/pitch 0.10, perspective 0, and limited depth (`:29`). These are prior reviewer observations, not new visual verification. This is direct evidence that geometry existence and rendered spatial communication must be scored separately.
6. **Repair is too late for an architectural dimensional mistake.** The gate has one findings-only review, at most one targeted patch, then a final verdict (`visual-gate.ts:70–120`). Repair preserves unaffected bytes and changes at most 25% (`scene-repair.md:3–11,32–34`). It wisely forbids deleting needed geometry, but cannot reliably replace a flat representation wholesale after narration/plan are fixed. Prevention belongs in planning and first scene authorship; preserve the repair limits during the experiment.
7. **Ambition has a tool boundary.** Capabilities recommends existing fixture catalogs and host-prepared surfaces (`shared/animlib/docs/capabilities.md:43–45,168–189`), but fresh `model-assets.json` defaults to an empty map (`generator.ts:57–60`). Production scene agents get no local filesystem tools. `publish_model` accepts public HTTPS GLB or generated named mesh data (`runtime.ts:192–202`), not direct native fixture lookup. Prompt-only experiments should use actual available helpers/generated geometry and describe unavailable preparation honestly.

## Two distinct prompt candidates

### A — Representation-loss decision

Goal: fix inappropriate dimensional choices by tightening the existing `view.rationale`, not by requiring 3D quotas.

- Planner: name the scene's claim, the spatial relationship it depends on, and the important information lost when flattened. Choose 3D when depth, enclosure, orientation, crossing, packing, curvature or spatial contact supplies evidence. Choose 2D when the inference is planar or the flat representation preserves every needed relation; “simpler/clearer/readable” alone is insufficient. A spatial subject name alone is also insufficient reason for 3D.
- Put the selected visible spatial evidence and any 3D-to-2D correspondence in `visualDescription`; preserve it in entity `meaning`/`endsWith` as needed. No schema additions.
- Editorial reviewer checks both directions: unjustified flattening and unnecessary depth. Flag a missing causal spatial relation as a material explanation failure, not a cosmetic preference.
- Scene author/reviewer implement/check the named relation in native frames. A 3D camera, rotation or generic spheres is not the evidence.

### B — Model-first visible commitment

Goal: fix underpowered 3D after the choice is already correct, by turning prose ambition into a concrete first-authoring obligation.

- Planner: for a spatial scene, state the recognizable primary mass, defining parts/negative space, their arrangement, the active operation and a frame where its consequence will be visible. Keep useful coarse context while enlarging the active region. Choose justified schematic fidelity explicitly; avoid a checklist of arbitrary extra parts.
- Scene author: construct the subject and shared kinematics first, then frame the operation so meaningful front/back overlap, thickness, valleys/cavities or relative depth remain legible. An oblique view, section or local move is selected only when needed to expose that named relation. Add annotations after the geometry communicates the operation. Use existing inspect/preview budgets.
- Reviewer: compare the rendered spatial commitment with the plan (silhouette, occupied volume/negative space, part arrangement, contact/motion, identity). A numerically deep mesh that reads as a slab fails the promised spatial evidence. Preserve successful detail in repair; annotate or reframe before simplifying.
- Preserve appropriate 2D scenes and purposeful flat subviews. These additions strengthen geometry/staging rather than dimensional-choice justification.

The parent selected an incremental comparison: baseline, A (routing), and A+B (spatial). Candidate A is saved under `routing/`; another experiment agent owns the spatial copy. Comparing A+B with A attributes differences to the added construction guidance. Keep the user request identical; inject candidates into the corresponding real stage system prompts or frozen prompt snapshots, never as a richer candidate-only topic request.

## Full-pipeline experiment

- Start every sample from a fresh UUID/data directory and the unchanged request: real `createPiGenerator` -> real draft/editorial review -> supported subtitle timing or paid narration -> every scene -> native visual review/repair. `generator.ts:91–115,185–190` reuses saved stages, so seeded lessons invalidate a planning comparison.
- Reuse/adapt `data/prompt-quality-comparison-20261011/run.ts` for its full-pipeline boundary. Its prior harness is Classic; use the current app's Interactive request mode for this evaluation so the main/subview contract matches production. Keep optional scene preview consistently enabled/disabled across variants. Subtitle mode tests the actual supported full subtitle path, not TTS/acoustic alignment.
- Do not use `showcase-trial.ts:82–94` or `quality-trial.ts:41–46` as end-to-end evidence: they seed/freeze lesson and narration.
- Minimum varied set: spatial biological mechanism (DNA opening or enzyme contact), spatial mathematical relation (two-input surface/gradient), planar control (2×2 matrix action or a sorting invariant), and mixed lesson with purposeful 3D-to-2D correspondence. At least two connected scenes expose handoff loss. Freeze durations/audience/coverage but do not request a dimension in ordinary routing cases.
- Blind reviewers see the same request plus corresponding operation samples and motion, not candidate names. Score separately: dimensional fitness; visible spatial evidence/recognizability; mechanism and scientific fidelity; identity through changes; teaching/reveal/readability; technical validity. Compare initial and final generated scenes to detect repair weakening. A 3D percentage, object count or successful compilation is never a quality score.
- Preserve failed runs and all draft/review/scene snapshots, hashes, metrics, first/second gate outcomes, model/thinking settings and generated source unchanged. Report completion failures as outcomes; no cherry-picked retries or coordinator scene polishing.

## Nested scene subagent support

Production Pi agents cannot spawn nested agents today. `runtime.ts:141–144` disables discovered extensions, skills, templates and local context. `runtime.ts:201–259` installs an explicit list of validation/submission, optional publication/inspection/preview, and web tools. There is no delegation tool. They have 12 turns (`runtime.ts:276`), four inspections and two optional preview batches (`runtime.ts:222,230,238,245`). The host already uses separate planner, editorial, scene, verifier and repair conversations. Codex experiment subagents can independently author candidates or judge results, but that is orchestration outside the production scene agent. A genuine nested scene specialist needs a host API/runtime change with bounded budget, shared asset/state contract and provenance; prompt wording alone cannot supply it.
