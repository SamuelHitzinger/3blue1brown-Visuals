# One chain, one folded shape

## Beat 1 — Follow the connected route

Content needed: Prepared 1CRN chain A residue beads and adjacency guides. One bead represents one amino acid, not one atom. Trace the deposited chain in sequence without moving any bead. Keep the whole connected route visible; withhold the envelope until beat-2.

Notes: Subtitles only; no voiceover. Assume the learner knows proteins are amino-acid chains but may picture a chain as stretched out. The missing intuition is that sequence connectivity and spatial arrangement are different properties. Enter through following a visible route. Two scenes, approximately 75–85 seconds including the thinking interval; reading-time estimate, not measured timing.

Narration: Here is crambin, a small protein with forty-six amino acids. Each bead stands for one amino acid. The links let us follow their order along the chain.

Narration: Watch the gold highlight travel from one end toward the other. The beads themselves stay still.

Invitation (spoken): Does the route keep heading away from its starting point, or turn back through the same region? Take a few seconds to look.

Pause: 4s

Reveal (spoken): It turns back! Coils and bends bring different stretches alongside each other, while the route stays connected.
