# Real-time visual style contract

Reference: approved Euler component and derivative frames in the user's
`3blue1brown-explanation-style` skill, plus task-matched saved baseline animations.
Root inspected both approved images and the rejected chart-template image.

The explanatory world owns the frame. Geometry, entities and data relationships
carry the reasoning; labels identify nearby objects. Stable neutral scaffolding
and a restrained palette connect objects to their terms. New evidence changes
the same construction, with purposeful movement and a readable endpoint.

For the initial cube mechanism, retain cube identity and shape through rotation,
then translation or color changes. Rotation must expose spatial structure rather
than decorate a title. For a curve, keep axes, curve and tracked point coherent
through movement and local tangent construction. General diagrams must show their
actual relation; generic boxes do not qualify when the evidence requires geometry.

For lecture-derived cases, infer coverage only from evidence seen so far and label
uncertainty. Do not silently invent missing values or replace a spatial construction
with a flat schematic unless the explanation warrants it. Model-only probe prompts
are capability tests, not full lessons or proof of style parity.

Reject missing geometry, detached annotations, unreadable scale, unexplained
equation changes, camera resets, identity loss, decorative motion and scene cards
that substitute for a causal construction. Runtime correctness and speed do not
establish visual quality. Fresh reviewers inspect actual native motion evidence.
