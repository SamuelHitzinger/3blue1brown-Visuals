## What this screen establishes

The fixtures do exercise meaningful complexity increases: two lines with linked projections versus one; 200 versus 3200 surface triangles with a moving slice; 14 low-detail heavy atoms versus 24 smoother atoms with explicit hydrogens and preserved double bonds. No rendered 3D case was a flat 2D/text-only substitute. Actual detail is therefore present, but higher geometry counts do not establish explanatory quality: surface labels still fail readability, and heavy B corrupts intermediate molecular geometry despite complete endpoint counts.

The strongest sampled mechanisms are line-basic B, wave B, heavy A and full A. Preferences are case-local randomized labels and must not be treated as model identities. Before claiming broader reliability, repair the identified defects and inspect representative dense motion or playback, especially every atom emphasis, surface sweep and vector cancellation. A larger/repeated screen is still needed for a general across-subject quality claim.
