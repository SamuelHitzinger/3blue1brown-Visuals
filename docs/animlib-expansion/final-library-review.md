## Cost and bounded remaining limitations

No speculative performance refactor is required for this acceptance. Costs and the scope of existing optimizations are visible:

- The renderer's reviewed authored-normal optimization preserves packed geometry/style ordering; its measurements do not promise universal native latency improvements.
- The optional peptide surface cache avoids recomputing a settled pose while camera and opacity continue. Its roughly 31% held-phase improvement is a measured CPU evaluation/serialization result, not GPU speed. The independent cache review checked exact frame equivalence. The much lighter current schematic proof is a separate accepted visual representation.
- Factories are still eagerly injected into the QuickJS bootstrap. Reports describe occasional 200 ms compile-deadline pressure under concurrent load and successful isolated reruns. That is a real scaling limitation, with causation not yet isolated; no deadline was raised to conceal it. Measure empty versus demanding scenes before changing factory loading.
- Long dynamic arrays, full-frame serialization, transparency sorting and finite-sample focus can remain expensive. Input ceilings are safety/validation limits, not interactive frame-rate guarantees. Host envelope partitioning does not increase the 32 MiB VM, scene/source/update, registry or focus budgets.

The accepted scientific/biological scope remains explicit: synthetic event rates and a fixed projection rather than measured or learned neural structure; a prescribed logistic parameter change rather than a training optimizer; compact queue choreography rather than a complete Dijkstra lesson; prescribed peptide folding rather than molecular dynamics; bounded transcription elongation without full termination/final closure; schematic cut/join without lariat chemistry; authored aperture transport rather than arbitrary collision avoidance; and standard-code translation without wobble, alternative codes or atomistic kinetics. Arbitrary font loading, full PBR/self-shadow realism and whole-channel near-identity are not established. None is silently counted as completed capability.

The latest independent visual reports accept the actual replacement films under the revised art target, with hashes and dense ordered sampling clearly distinguished from uninterrupted realtime playback. A correct codec or API test does not substitute for those visual gates. Changes to geometry, choreography, labels or camera after this freeze require the affected numerical and visual checks again.
