## Reusable capability implications

These are implementation requirements inferred from the inspected motion, not
claims that current library already matches the studio output.

- Maintain stable base, residue and strand identities across wide/close views,
  selection desaturation, local labels and molecular representation changes.
- Prepare reusable organic meshes with real openings and irregular silhouettes.
  Translucency must expose internal channels while preserving front/back depth.
  Geometric spheres alone cannot establish this visual capability.
- Support explicit docking trajectories and absolute-time phase boundaries:
  approach, orient, contact, form link, release. Mechanical identity persists
  through each phase. Unrelated random motion cannot substitute for contact.
- Provide bounded curve deformation, local strand separation, growing connected
  chains, triplet groups, and topology handoff for splice/rejoin/release events.
- Treat environment and cinematic rendering separately: layered soft-focus
  membranes/particles, controlled depth contrast, smooth camera travel and
  selective focus. Their absence remains a style gap even if molecular geometry
  and motion tests pass.
- Keep this saturated organic style separate from Protein Folding's scientific
  representations, transcription's edited instructional style, and dark flat
  mathematical explainers. Artist profiles belong in scenes/palettes.
- Use authoritative molecular data for biological conventions. Visual lettering
  and illustrative timing in a source film are not biochemical ground truth.

Current next slice addresses nucleic geometry, incorporation phases and real
structure identity. Environmental focus, splicing, pore transit and translation
must receive explicit later acceptance or remain recorded gaps; this evidence is
not a whole-library fidelity approval.
