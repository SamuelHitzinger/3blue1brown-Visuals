Two concrete revisions remain:

1. **Pore label placement, 3.5–5.5 s, both aspects.** In native 4.649 s at 1280×720, `mRNA` lies at approximately x584–630/y400–414 while the green shaft passes through its right-hand letters. At 4.65 s the label remains pressed against the shaft/lobe junction (approximately x598–641/y477–491). The 4:3 shot has the same overlap translated left. Because it is a small white overlay label with no leader, its word shape and object boundary merge. Offset the label into clear membrane space, add a short unobtrusive leader if needed, and recheck dense frames on both sides of the cut. This is distinct from the surface-depth finding.
2. **Pore spatial rendering, 0–10 s.** Source natives 153/158 show a broad membrane with soft illumination gradients, a dark lumen, contact shadows and pores integrated into the surface; its low view makes the green chain's passage across depth immediate. Candidate native 4.65 s has a mostly uniform lavender field, an opening whose visible interior is very close to the surrounding field, isolated elongated orange columns, and sharply conspicuous grey context pores clipped by the top/side borders. Real mesh holes are present, but the rendered depth evidence is substantially weaker. Improve surface/lumen/contact lighting and soften/recompose context pores while retaining the verified hole, chain, hold and cut. Recheck 4.4–6.5 s and the final rising arc at both aspects against source 153/158. This is a visual fidelity issue; additional proof of hidden mesh topology or the accepted optical-focus implementation does not resolve it.
