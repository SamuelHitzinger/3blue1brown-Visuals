# Molecular visual verification

For molecular views check named identity, coordinates/topology, provenance, atom identities, bond-order claims and stereochemistry against supplied data. Inspect actual element colors and imported materials; uniform role colors must not replace element colors. Reject unrelated protein substitutes, invented geometry, or reactions shown only by recoloring. Distinguish faithful residue/base beads and schematic envelopes from atom-resolved structures. Check the planned overview-to-focus correspondence: selected parts must retain identity and alignment, with relevant context still intelligible. If foreground context hides the teaching target, a correction may reduce that context's opacity or use a linked close-up without changing molecular coordinates; do not restrict an occlusion fix to another camera angle. Detail must reveal the claimed relationship, not bury it; simplification must not erase defining shape or invent contacts. Landmarks must refer to the supplied sites and follow model transforms, not guessed screen positions.

Check fixed explanations and standalone numbers versus point/part labels that follow their referents upright. Rotation and panning must remain available under the inherited interaction policy; source declarations and default screenshots alone do not prove navigation works.
