# Molecular scene craft

For molecular geometry, use verified coordinates and topology or suitable published assets. Preserve identity, sequence, connectivity, stereochemistry, units, and scientifically justified conformation through motion. At atomic resolution preserve element colors: C gray, H white, N blue, O red, S yellow, P orange. Uniform material fills must not hide imported atom colors; mild identity tints must leave elements recognizable. A reaction changes the appropriate bonds or atom mapping, not just labels/colors. Never invent coordinates, provenance, or a substitute protein. Use publish_model for required preparation when available.

Implement the plan's molecular representation choice, preserving its evidence. Keep detailed whole-complex geometry when arrangement matters; focus or hide named parts when they obscure the current inference. Use same-source envelopes for outer shape, residue traces/beads for chain organization, and local atoms/sticks for supported chemistry. Do not substitute bundled crambin for another molecule or replace distinctive structure with a generic blob. Residue beads are not atoms; smoothed envelopes are schematic; traces cannot establish exact surface contact; sticks without encoded bond order cannot establish bond order.

Before an observation cue, check that the target itself is distinguishable at delivery size. If whole-complex detail hides it, reduce nonessential foreground opacity or open a linked close-up while preserving the relevant context and coordinates. Changing viewpoint repeatedly or adding a leader into an opaque mass does not establish visibility.

Map overview and focus through the same identities and coordinate frame. Prefer selecting parts of one published model; if separate assets have different origins, align their source frames before overlay. Keep enough context to show where a close-up came from and offer meaningful inspection without requiring interaction for the explanation. Use supplied molecular landmarks for attached labels or camera targets, applying model transforms once; these sites are not inferred active sites. A representation transition reveals different information about one structure, not measured folding or processive motion.
